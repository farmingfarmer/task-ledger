Runs on this machine only · suggests, never decides · keeps score on itself, not on you — the deal
  • Everything runs here. Transcripts, models, database — nothing is sent to any cloud, ever.
  • It only reads what someone deliberately gave it. No microphones, no live capture. A transcript has to be handed over.
  • What it heard about you is yours alone until you confirm it. Unreviewed claims are never shown to anyone else.
  • Nothing reaches a board without a named person saying yes. It suggests; people decide. There is no other path.
  • Every fact keeps its receipt — the sentence, the speaker, the meeting, the hash. Nothing here is a summary's opinion.
  • Wrong about something? One keystroke corrects it, the correction is permanent, and it is scored against the system — not against you.
  • There is no scorecard of people, and there never will be. It tracks work, not workers.
